## 1. Getting Started — Creating Your Account

To access all GCLT services, you need a registered account. Creating one is free and takes less than a minute.

### Option A: Register with Email & Password

1. Go to the GCLT website and click **"Get Started"** or **"Register"**.
2. Enter your **Full Name**, **Email Address**, and a secure **Password**.
3. Click **"Create Account"**.
4. Check your email inbox for a **verification link** from GCLT and click it to activate your account.

> **Important:** You must verify your email before you can submit bookings or request quotes.

### Option B: Sign In with Google

1. On the Login page, click **"Continue with Google"**.
2. Select your Google account.
3. Your account is automatically created and verified — no email confirmation needed.

## 4. Booking a Transport (Step-by-Step)

This is the core feature of the GCLT platform. Booking a truck involves three simple steps.

### How to Start

1. From the sidebar, click **"Book Transport"**.
2. Follow the 3-step booking wizard shown at the top of the page.

---

### Step 1: Route & Schedule

This is where you tell us where your cargo is going and when.

#### Pickup Location

- Enter the **Street / Building / Landmark** (e.g., "SBMA Port Gate 3").
- Enter the **Barangay** (optional but recommended).
- Enter the **City / Municipality** (required, e.g., "Olongapo City").

**Shortcut options:**
- **"Use My Location"** — Click this button to automatically fill in your current GPS location. Your browser will ask for permission; click "Allow".
- **"Pin on Map"** — Opens an interactive map. Click anywhere on the map to select your pickup point.

#### Drop-off Location

Fill in the delivery address using the same fields above.

> **Note:** Pickup and drop-off cannot be the same location. The system will warn you if they match.

#### Date & Time

- Choose your preferred **date** (today or any future date).
- Choose your preferred **pickup time**.

#### Cargo Type

Select the type of cargo you are shipping:

| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| **General** | Standard dry cargo, pallets, commercial goods |
| **Refrigerated** | Perishable items requiring temperature control |
| **Hazardous** | Chemicals or materials requiring safety handling |
| **Oversized** | Heavy machinery or items with large dimensions |

#### Cargo Weight & Dimensions

- Enter the **weight in kilograms** (e.g., `5000`).
- Enter the **Length, Width, and Height in meters** (optional but helps match the right vehicle).

#### Route Assignment (Automatic)

Based on your cargo details, the system will automatically assign one of two routes:

- 🚀 **Expressway Route** — Assigned for standard cargo under 3,000 KG. Faster transit.
- 🛣️ **Old Road Route** — Assigned for heavy or oversized cargo (3,000 KG and above). Safer for heavy loads.

The assigned route is displayed in a colored alert below the form. You cannot change this — it is determined by GCLT's load compliance standards.

#### Live Map Preview

On the right side of the screen, an interactive map shows your route. As you fill in pickup and drop-off addresses, the map updates with pins and calculates the **estimated travel distance in kilometers**.

Click **"Continue to Vehicle"** when all required fields (*) are filled.

---

### Step 2: Choose Your Vehicle

Browse the available truck categories and select the one best suited to your cargo.

#### How to Choose

- Trucks are shown with their **payload capacity**, **dimensions**, and **rate per kilometer**.
- The **first truck listed** is automatically recommended based on your cargo specifications.
- Trucks that **cannot carry your cargo** are greyed out and marked **"Insufficient Capacity"** — you cannot select them.

#### Fleet Categories

| Category | Capacity | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| 🛻 Small Trucks | Up to 2 tons | Local multi-drop, light commercial deliveries |
| 🚛 Medium Trucks | 2 – 5 tons | Commercial freight, medium-distance routes |
| 🚚 Large Trucks | 5 – 15 tons | Heavy industrial and port cargo |
| 🏗️ Specialized | Varies | Refrigerated, flatbed, tanker transport |

Use the **filter tabs** at the top to show only one category at a time.

Click on a truck to select it. The card will show a green **"✓ Selected"** badge.

Click **"Continue to Review"** when a vehicle is selected.

---

### Step 3: Review & Confirm

Before submitting, review all your booking details:

- **Route Summary** — Pickup and drop-off addresses, date, time, cargo type, weight, dimensions, and auto-assigned route.
- **Selected Vehicle** — The truck you chose with its category and capacity.
- **Special Instructions** — Add any important notes for the driver or logistics team:
  - Gate pass requirements (e.g., *"Gate pass needed at SBMA Zone 1"*)
  - Fragile handling instructions
  - Contact person at the drop-off point
  - Any specific loading/unloading requirements

**Cost Summary** — Pricing is not set at this stage. The GCLT team will calculate the exact cost based on your route, cargo, and vehicle selection.

Click **"Request a Quote"** to submit.

---

### What Happens After You Submit?

1. Your booking is created with the status **"Quote Requested"**.
2. You receive a **confirmation email** at your registered address.
3. A **dashboard notification** confirms your submission.
4. The GCLT logistics team reviews your booking and sends you a **pricing quote within 2–4 hours** (during business hours).

## 5. Tracking Your Bookings

To check the status of any booking:

1. Click **"My Bookings"** in the sidebar.
2. All your bookings are listed with their current status and details.

### Booking Status Explained

| Status | What It Means |
|---|---|
| **Quote Requested** | Your request was submitted. The GCLT team is reviewing it. |
| **Quoted** | GCLT has calculated the price. Check your notifications for the amount. |
| **Pending Payment** | You accepted the quote. Complete your payment to confirm the booking. |
| **Confirmed** | Payment is received. Your transport is officially scheduled. |
| **In Transit** | Your cargo is on its way. |
| **Completed** | Delivery was successfully completed. |
| **Cancelled** | The booking was cancelled (by you or GCLT). |
| **Declined** | The booking was declined (e.g., availability or compliance issue). |

You will receive a **notification** every time your booking status changes.

## 6. Paying for Your Booking

Once your booking is **Quoted**, you can review the price and choose a payment method.

### Option A: Online Payment (Stripe)

- Pay using a **credit card or debit card** securely via Stripe.
- Stripe is a globally trusted payment platform. All transactions are encrypted.
- Once payment is confirmed, your booking status automatically changes to **"Confirmed"**.
- You receive a payment receipt email.

### Option B: Cash on Delivery (COD)

- Select Cash if you prefer to pay when the cargo is delivered (or upon inspection for truck purchases).
- Once your cargo is received and you make payment, the **driver or your representative uploads a photo** of the cash receipt through the platform.
- The GCLT admin reviews the uploaded receipt image to confirm payment.
- Your booking status is then updated to **"Confirmed"** or **"Completed"**.

## 8. Scheduling a Truck Viewing

If you are interested in purchasing a truck, you can schedule an on-site inspection at the GCLT yard.

1. Open the truck detail page for the vehicle you want to inspect.
2. Click **"Schedule Viewing"** or **"Book Viewing"**.
3. Fill in the viewing form:
   - Your **Full Name** and **Contact Number**
   - Your **Current Office/Base Location**
   - **Preferred Date** and **Preferred Time** (Mon–Sat, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM)
   - **Special Requests** (e.g., request for a mechanical inspection or test start)
4. Click **"Schedule Appointment"** to submit.

The GCLT sales team will review your request and confirm your appointment. You will receive a notification and email confirmation.

## 12. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I need an account to browse trucks for sale?**  
A: No. The truck sales catalog is publicly accessible. However, you need an account to schedule a viewing or submit a booking.

**Q: How long does it take to get a quote?**  
A: The GCLT team typically responds within **2–4 business hours** during weekdays (8:00 AM – 5:00 PM, Monday to Saturday).

**Q: Can I cancel my booking?**  
A: Yes. You can request a cancellation from the **My Bookings** screen while the booking is in `Quote Requested` or `Quoted` status. Contact GCLT directly for bookings already in `Confirmed` or `In Transit` status.

**Q: What areas does GCLT service?**  
A: GCLT services the **entire Philippines**, with primary operations in SBMA, Olongapo City, and Central Luzon. Long-haul nationwide transport is available.
